# Company documents

The documents a data scientist would write at each stage of the experiment, as they would circulate inside the company. They follow the chapters of the [case study](../../CASE_STUDY.md), which tells the same story in one place.

The data and results are real. The company, its people and its business figures (margins, costs, file size, sign-offs) are an invented scenario; people are referred to by role only.

| Chapter | Document | Who it's for |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | [The request](ch1a_request.md) | The email from Marketing, with the CFO's doubt, that starts the project |
| 1 | [Kickoff notes](ch1b_kickoff_notes.md) | The questions that turn a request into a testable plan |
| 2 | [Experiment design doc](ch2_experiment_design_doc.md) | Stakeholders signing off the design before launch |
| 3 | [Validation report](ch3_validation_report.md) | The go / no-go before anyone reads results |
| 4 | [Results readout](ch4_results_readout.md) | Marketing and Finance: what happened, against the pre-agreed rules |
| 5 | [Targeting analysis](ch5_targeting_analysis.md) | Marketing: who should get which email |
